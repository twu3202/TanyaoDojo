# Longyan Mahjong

The [Longyan New Chapter tables and payment chart](https://www.ourgame.com/game/game-intro-new/df/fj/h2g/lyxzmj_002.html)
describe the main room. The [Half Self-Draw operator rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/1114.html) describe
the second room's draw and robbery gate, natural Thirteen Orphans, passed kongs, and gold-discard restrictions.
Both Dojo rooms use four seats, eight hands, all three numbered suits and a fixed twelve-tile reserve.

## Tiles and opening

There are 144 tiles with seven ordinary honors and eight flowers. Deal thirteen tiles plus one to the dealer.
Replace flowers in seat order, finishing a pass before replacing any newly drawn flowers. Take replacements
from the same end as ordinary draws. Open the first nonflower there as gold, exposing any intervening flowers
for the dealer. Gold has only three available wildcard copies because the physical indicator stays visible.
When a different face is gold, White Dragons fill its printed face in concealed scoring. Calls use printed
faces, so a natural White Dragon can be called as White Dragon. Gold itself cannot be called.

Both rooms allow pon and kong without chows. The New Chapter opening asks any seat holding the three actual
gold tiles first. Otherwise a nondealer can add the indicator to a thirteen-tile hand, or the dealer can replace
one non-gold tile with it. The closest nondealer takes priority over the dealer, and only one seat wins.
Half Self-Draw asks for a completed dealer opening win first. A separately declared gold claim earns four
points. The physical indicator cannot count toward an opening three-gold declaration.

## Traveling gold

On a draw, discard a tile that leaves a gold wildcard free to pair with any draw, with all other sets complete.
A seven-pairs hand can use six completed pairs and one free gold instead. Declare with the specific discard.
On the next draw you can win the travel or use a newly drawn gold for the next level by discarding it while
keeping the same any-tile wait. An ordinary discard by the traveler ends the travel. A replacement draw can
finish the travel. One player has the active travel at a time. The declaration is offered with at least four
live tiles. Double travel limits other seats to ordinary draws and self-draw wins. In triple travel the others
can also use concealed kongs, with wins restricted to concealed-kong replacements.

The latest gold discard imposes restrictions on opponents until that player discards again, keeping the
window active if that discard is another gold. New Chapter restricts opponents to self-draw wins. Half
Self-Draw also blocks their pons. After one gold, it allows three-gold or travel wins, kong replacements,
and natural Thirteen Orphans on a robbed added kong. After consecutive gold throws it permits only
concealed-kong replacement wins until the window clears. Half Self-Draw records a declined direct kong by
face for the rest of the hand, including when the player chooses pon. In New Chapter the player may add
the fourth tile later as usual.

## Settlement

| Win | Base per opponent |
| --- | ---: |
| New Chapter discard win | 1, with 1 more from the supplier |
| Ordinary self-draw or added-kong robbery | 2 |
| Gold claim, three golds, or single travel | 4 |
| Double travel or dealer opening win | 8 |
| Triple travel or Half Self-Draw natural Thirteen Orphans | 16 |

Any ordinary bill involving the dealer doubles. The New Chapter supplier's extra discard point is added after
doubling. In the Half Self-Draw room the robbed kong owner alone pays 12 if the winner or owner is the
dealer, or 8 between nondealers. These values follow the operator's four-player robbery examples.
The Half Self-Draw room uses the New Chapter award schedule for opening and travel wins.
Both rooms award only one winner in turn order on an offer.

An exposed kong pays one per opponent, a concealed kong pays two, and payments involving the dealer double.
These payments occur once the kong completes. A robbed kong earns no kong points. In New Chapter, the fourth
public flower and each later flower pays one per opponent with dealer doubling. In Half Self-Draw, the four
plants and the four seasons count separately, paying once for each completed group. Draws keep these transfers.

At the opening, each nondealer pays nothing for matching the dealer. When all four discard the same printed
face, the dealer pays every other seat two for a numbered tile or one for an honor. Each subsequent consecutive
match doubles that amount. A mismatch or a call ends the streak. The dealer keeps the seat after a draw or a
dealer win. Otherwise the next seat deals.
