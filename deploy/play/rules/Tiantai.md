# Tiantai Three-Player Mahjong · 300 Hu

The table uses 108 tiles. There are four copies each of the character, circle and seven honor faces, and
eight distinct seasons and plants. The three seats are East, South and West. Deal 13 per seat and a 14th to
the dealer. Set aside 15 tiles at the replacement end. Reveal flowers as they enter play, including during
the deal, and replace them at the edge of the reserve. Kongs also draw there. When a replacement chain
exhausts the live wall, settle a draw, except that the eighth flower can still complete an Eight Flowers win.

This room uses the no-wildcard option and the calls listed in the operator's guide, pon and kong. A pass on
a win or pon blocks that move until the player's next draw or successful call. Allow multiple winners on
one discard or added kong. The final discard can only be claimed to win. A lone dealer win keeps the dealer.
A non-dealer win or a draw passes the deal one seat clockwise. Play eight hands.

## Calculating hu and tai

A standard hand has four sets and a pair. Start its hu at 20, add four per flower and two for self-draw.
The table uses the highest-scoring legal decomposition and winning-tile placement. A pair of the seat
wind or a dragon adds two. A pair, inside or edge wait adds two. Under this room's placement convention,
a discard that completes an undeclared pung scores that pung as open. The score view lists the resulting
base hu and total tai.

| Set | Ordinary suited tile | Terminal, wind or dragon |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Concealed kong | 16 | 32 |
| Open or added kong | 8 | 16 |
| Concealed pung | 4 | 8 |
| Open pung | 2 | 4 for a terminal, the seat wind or a dragon. Other winds score 2. |

Add one tai for each matching flower, such as Spring and Plum for East. Add one for a complete set of
seasons or plants. Each seat-wind or dragon pung or kong adds one. All pungs, half flush, and all sequences
with an unvalued pair and a two-sided wait each add one. Pure one suit adds three. Robbery, a self-draw
after a flower replacement or kong, and a final live self-draw each add one.

Multiply base hu by two per tai. This room rounds upward to a multiple of ten and caps at 300. Heavenly
Hand and Eight Flowers pay 300. Earth pays 150 and applies to the dealer's opening discard or a
non-dealer's first uninterrupted draw. The operator's online rounding example and transfer description
are incomplete. This room uses the full share from each opponent on self-draw, or from the supplier to
each winner on a discard or robbed kong. A draw pays zero.
