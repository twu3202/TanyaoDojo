# Changbai and Chaoyang Town Mahjong

Both rooms use 136 tiles, four seats and eight hands. An eligible discard may have several winners.
The dealer repeats on a draw or when first in claim order among the winners, and otherwise the
seat passes to the next player.

## Changbai Piao and Single Wait

[Bianfeng's Changbai rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/321.html) describe a pre-deal piao choice,
one-tile ready declarations, multiple discard winners, and a tail that changes after any kong.
Each player chooses 0, 2, 4 or 6 before any hand is dealt. Choices are disclosed together. Each
losing bill receives an additive amount equal to the winner's and payer's choices.

A player can declare only a single legal wait. A declared player may preserve that wait with a
concealed, added or fed kong. A win requires a prior declaration. Standard hands embrace all suits,
and seven pairs needs an honor or terminal. Thirteen orphans starts at 13. A side, middle or pair
wait adds one point to the ordinary base. A single terminal or honor pair wait earns another fan.
Both a pure suit and mixed one suit earn two fan on a win. An open all-pung hand doubles the
base and a closed one quadruples it. The latter choice resolves a scoring range in the operator table.

Four matching non-kong numbered tiles in the winning hand and sets score one fan per face. Four
publicly present copies can also raise a losing bill. A claimed winning discard is public, and other
concealed tiles are not. Treat two or three identical chows similarly, using the highest repeat
value for each sequence. A closed winner adds one fan unless the hand is seven pairs. Score
dealer involvement and a closed payer separately. The Dojo room uses one bill per winner on a
discard and three bills for a self-draw. These settlement choices and which patterns stack resolve
unspecified details in the source.

Regular open or added kongs pay 1 from each opponent and concealed kongs pay 2. Kong payments
are kept on a draw. With no kong the last 14 tiles are reserved, and the final four live tiles
are taken without discarding. The first completed kong releases the reserve, and the last four
wall tiles then form the split. On a draw the dealer pays each other seat 2, independently of piao.

## Chaoyang Town Eggs and Opposite End

[Bianfeng's Chaoyang Town rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/1045.html) describe the 136-tile
three-suit rack, eggs, their extensions and scoring, and the opposite-end feeding rule. This room
uses the closed-win, opening-only egg, missing-egg wait, and normal single-payer discard settings.
It allows multiple winners. A terminal or honor and a pung, kong, whole egg, or dragon pair are
required in addition to all three suits.

Reveal dragons, four winds, the three ones, or the three nines before the first discard. The
wind egg takes a replacement and the three-tile eggs keep their original tile count. A drawn
member can extend an existing egg. Its fourth or later tile is a public offer for other players
to win or pon. A won or stolen extension is unpaid and still leaves its original egg unchanged.
For a missing-egg win, dragons, ones, and nines may make a set once the missing tile completes
the group. The synthetic set affects the hand shape and has no separately paid kong.

On chow or pon the taken face cannot be thrown immediately. If a chow takes an end, the next
discard of the adjacent tile at its opposite end can carry liability. A fed win or direct kong
on that discard transfers the total of the three calculated bills to the discarder. This room
keeps separate ordinary kong bills, with one dealer factor per bill. Ordinary direct or added
kongs start at 1, selected faces start at 2, 4 or 8, and concealed kongs double their base.
An opening egg starts at 4 and each completed extension at 1. A supplier who can win on the
tile they fed to an accepted direct kong may reclaim it instead, treating the would-be konger
as the robbed payer. This occurs only after ordinary discard win responses resolve.

An all-pung win starts at 8, an edge, middle, pair, lone-terminal or missing-egg wait at 2,
and other wins at 1. In a non-all-pung hand, winning on a concealed pung while keeping
dragon eyes adds two fan as ding. If both pairs were dragons it adds four. Evaluate compatible
decompositions and use the highest score. Three or four open sets increase the winning base
by one or two fan. Count dealer, closed hand, three closed opponents, self-draw and supply
factors separately as applicable. A successful robbery adds another factor for its supplier.

Only self-draw or pass is available on each final split tile. When a kong consumes a tile at the
preceding discard boundary, its replacement serves as the first remaining split draw. This
room choice keeps the physical wall and remaining split opportunities aligned.
