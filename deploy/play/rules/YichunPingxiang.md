# Yichun and Pingxiang Mahjong

Both four-seat rooms use all 136 printed tiles. Dealer and guests start with fourteen and thirteen tiles, respectively.
A table plays eight hands. The live wall can be drawn to exhaustion and a kong needs a replacement tile.

## Yichun Honor Chows

[Tongcheng's Yichun table guide](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d26980.html) allows three different winds or the three dragons
to form a chow, in the rack or as a call from the previous seat. Besides four sets and a pair and seven pairs, the room
accepts fourteen separated tiles. In a suit there must be room for two ranks between any two kept ranks, as with
1, 4 and 7. Each honor must be unique in a separated hand. All seven honors make the seven-star version.

An all-honor rack can win even without a standard shape. It pays 6, or 12 if the same tiles also form four sets
and a pair. [Tongcheng's pattern multipliers](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d32008.html) also give 2 for separated,
3 for seven-star or all pungs, 4 for seven pairs and progressive doubling for each printed quad in seven pairs.
A pure numbered standard hand multiplies its shape by 5. The highest eligible shape score applies.
A valid opening self-draw or win on the dealer's opening discard pays the standalone 8-point opening award.
In the Dojo schedule, closed hands other than the two all-honor awards, a single-tile wait with four exposed sets,
winning on a kong-related tile, and winning with the final live tile each double the bill.
An added kong can be robbed. A concealed kong does not interrupt closed-hand status.

Several players may win on the same discard. The discarder or robbed-kong owner pays each winner separately.
On a self-draw every opponent pays. Passing a discard win blocks that face until the player next draws or makes
a successful call. The dealer stays on a draw, the sole winner deals next, and the supplier deals after multiple wins.
A direct kong charges the supplier 1, an added kong charges each opponent 1, and a concealed kong charges each
opponent 2. At exhaustion, the final discard can still win and all kongs are refunded on a draw.
The final tile remains in play as described in [Tongcheng's final-tile and draw guide](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d11415.html).

## Pingxiang True Pair

[Tongcheng's Pingxiang table guide](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d26981.html) requires 2, 5 or 8 as the pair in a basic win.
A basic hand wins by self-draw and cannot include a declared chow. Chows from the previous seat are available,
but after any chow the player must win with a pure numbered suit. A pure standard hand or seven pairs does not
need 2/5/8. A pungs hand in multiple suits needs 2/5/8. Bigger hands can claim a discard or rob an added kong.
The first eligible claimant in turn order wins. A legal 2/5/8 basic hand can claim the dealer's opening discard for Earthly Win.

The numerical schedule follows [Tongcheng's hand tiers](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d32108.html).
The base is 1, seven pairs or a pure numbered hand or all pungs with true eyes pays 4,
pure suit with true eyes pays 8, and pure pungs with true eyes pays 16. One quad in seven pairs, pure seven pairs,
and all-honor seven pairs pay at least 8. Two quads in seven pairs pay 16 by room convention.
Three quads in seven pairs, Heavenly Win and Earthly Win pay 32.

The discarder or robbed-kong owner pays the normal discard bill, while a self-draw charges all three opponents.
After supplying three chows in the same suit, the supplier covers the full self-draw bill or the single discard bill.
Robbing an added kong keeps its bill with the kong owner. A direct or added kong charges the supplier of the
claimed tile or original pung 1. A concealed kong charges each opponent 1. Every completed kong counts when
there is a winner, and all kong charges are canceled on a draw.

If the four first discards show the same face, the dealer pays each guest 1. When the wall runs out and nobody wins,
the dealer receives 1 from each guest if the dealer has at least one structural wait under the self-draw rules,
using the dealer's rack and melds. The ready-dealer award is separate from the opening match and kong ledger.
These amounts and the choice to play out the final discard are room conventions for the
[operator's matching-discard, feed and draw rules](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d36221.html).
The winner deals next after a win. The next player after the dealer deals on a draw.
