# Jilin City and Shuangliao Mahjong

## Jilin City Dice Bao

[Xinyue's scoring table](https://xinyueyouxi.com/game/16-2?doc-innerlink-PvP=region&region=) supplies the capped
winning and kong schedule. [Zadi describes the ready gates and dice](https://www.zadiqp.com/rules/jilinmajiang.html).
[Tiandu describes the matching-bao win](https://www.tdqp8.com/Home/Info/5473.html).

Deal 13/14 from the 136-tile wall. Play eight hands with one winner per hand. The dealer stays on a dealer win
or draw and advances one seat otherwise. Chows come from the preceding seat. An announced wait requires
all three numbered suits or one numbered suit with optional honors, and a terminal or wind in the rack or
melds. For four sets and a pair, there must be a pung or kong, or wind eyes. The winning tile qualifies as a
middle tile, edge tile, eyes, all pungs, or the final tile with four declared sets. Seven pairs can also win.
A quad can supply two pairs. The dealer's initial win and the win on the first discard can skip the declaration.

The first seat to declare ready opens bao before its next draw. Play private bao with six candidate positions
in the reserved wall, equivalent to a six-sided roll repeated for an ineligible position. Other ready seats
see the current bao before their own draws. Three public copies retire the face. Reroll for a previously
unused eligible position and keep the old tile in the reserve. Matching the waiting face on the initial
roll wins as 通宝. Matching after reopening uses 对宝 at the same multiplier. Once a player has seen the
current bao, a self-drawn bao face or One Bamboo can supply an announced wait. A discard keeps its face.

Start at 1 for middle, single-eyes or four declared sets, 2 for edge, 4 for all pungs and 16 for seven pairs.
Double for self-draw, single suit or four declared sets. Drawn bao adds a ×4 multiplier. Matching bao before
the draw uses ×8 without the self-draw multiplier. Each opponent pays, doubling a bill for the dealer's
involvement, for being the supplier, and once when either side is closed. Win payments cap at 16 per payer.

The room uses three distinct winds in a wind egg and the current shared egg procedure for dragons, ones,
nines and One Bamboo substitution. It pays 3 for forming an egg and 1 for each extension. Ordinary open
and concealed kongs pay 1/2 per opponent, One Dot pays 2/4, Two Dots pays 4/8, and Eight Characters and
One Bamboo pay 8/16. The 16-cap schedule, private bao with One Bamboo enabled, and the described closed
bill interpretation are this room's fixed choices.

## Shuangliao Pung Multipliers

[Xinyue publishes Shuangliao's payment multipliers](https://mobile.xinyueyouxi.com/game/140-2?region=&version=2).
Its public table names the categories. This room fixes the following play and category definitions where the
public table gives a multiplier alone.

Deal 13/14 from a 136-tile wall with a 14-tile reserve. Win with four sets and a pair, by discard, draw, or
added-kong robbery. Chows come from the preceding seat. The table has one winner, eight hands, and no
mandatory declaration. The winner deals the next hand and the dealer stays on a draw. A common win
starts at 1. An all-pung hand pays ×4. The 123-as-pung option treats sequences starting at 1 as pungs for
this award only if at least one set is a real pung or kong. The ×16 飘中飘 award requires a closed hand
with four real pungs or kongs. It takes the place of the ×4 award.

Double the result for self-draw, a closed standing hand, at least one One Bamboo, or a sole middle-tile wait.
A kong-replacement win or four copies split outside a declared kong doubles once. Robbing an added kong
doubles once. All opponents pay, with a further ×2 for dealer involvement and ×2 for the supplier of a winning
discard or robbed kong. Each opponent pays 2/4 for an open/concealed kong and 4/8 for a big kong of One Dot,
One Bamboo or a dragon. Kong payments survive a draw.
