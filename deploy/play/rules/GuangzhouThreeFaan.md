# Guangzhou Three-Faan Mahjong / 广州三番封顶麻将

Rule ID `guangzhou-three-faan-dojo-v1`. [Guangzhou table rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/164.html),
[Bianfeng three-faan base table](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/489.html).

- Four players, 136 tiles without flowers. Chow from the previous seat. Pong and kong outrank
  chow, and winning outranks a meld. Standard four-set-and-a-pair hands and thirteen orphans
  may win. Passing an offered win locks that tile until the player next draws or successfully
  claims any meld. The last live draw cannot be used to start a kong, and only wins can claim
  the last discard.
- The operator's basic table gives chicken hand 0 faan, all sequences 1, all triplets 2 and
  half flush 2. This room assigns pure suit and all honors 3, adds ordinary patterns up to 3,
  and uses the best legal decomposition. Opening heaven/earth/human wins are 6. The first
  uninterrupted draw of each non-dealer may be an earth win; a win on the first discard is
  a human win. This room assigns 6 to thirteen orphans and big three dragons, and 8 to big
  four winds, each as a standalone score.
- Each share is two to the power of the faan. Self-draw normally bills each opponent one share
  and a discarder pays one share to each winner. A triple discard win increases each winner
  to at least three faan and the discarder pays all three separately. A replacement self-draw,
  last-draw win or robbed kong also uses at least three faan. Only thirteen orphans can rob
  a concealed kong. Robbed kong bills the kong maker three shares per winner.
- A direct-kong replacement win is covered by that kong supplier. Other self-draws may
  be covered by the supplier of the fourth exposed meld, third exposed dragon pung or
  fourth exposed wind pung, when the winning hand satisfies that condition. Use the earliest
  applicable recorded liability. Each supplier can be held for only their first four-open-meld
  obligation to another player.
- The first dealer is randomized. A dealer who wins, including as part of a multiple win,
  repeats; otherwise the deal advances to the next seat, including on a draw. The room
  uses win payments only and makes no separate transfers for kongs.
