# Shenyang and Fuxin Mahjong

All three four-player rooms deal 13/14 from the 136-tile set and play eight hands. Each hand has a
single winner. A win passes the next deal to the winner, and a draw keeps the dealer. Ready marks
the wait and locks the rack. A ready player can kong with the drawn tile if the wait stays unchanged.

## Shenyang Whirlwind Kongs

[Pook publishes the open-hand rules, last four draws and fan](https://www.pook.com/games/symj.html).
Open via chow, pon or open kong. A whirlwind or a concealed kong alone leaves the hand closed.
Use all three suits, at least one terminal or honor, and a pung or kong unless the eyes are dragons.
Announcing ready is optional. The first 14 tiles can reveal a four-wind or three-dragon whirlwind.
Each takes one set. Take a replacement for the four-wind set. The three-dragon set takes none.
An extra wind can extend the four-wind group before that seat's first discard. The shown whirlwind
counts as a pung and as an honor for shape eligibility. It contributes two fan upon winning.

Every exposed pon of a face present in another seat's whirlwind earns one more fan. The check uses
the winning table, so showing the whirlwind after the pon also counts. Dragon pungs already earn one
fan. Exposed and concealed kongs count one and two fan respectively, with dragon kongs adding one.
Kong points are included in the winning bill. The final four wall tiles go to players in turn. Each
recipient can win or keep the tile and pass. These draws produce no discard and no claim window.

Start at zero base fan and charge 2 to the power of the fan. Self-draw, a named single wait, the final
split draw, kong replacement, robbing an added kong, a winning discard following a kong, and four equal
tiles outside a kong can add one fan each. An all-pung hand adds three. A bill involving the dealer adds
one. A closed opponent adds one, or two when all three opponents are closed. Only the supplier pays on a
discard or robbery, adding one fan to their bill. All opponents pay on a self-draw. This room chooses a
64-point cap per payer, no account-balance limit, and no independent kong payout. The final four draws
are reserved for self-draws once reached.

## Fuxin Middle Five

[Bianfeng publishes the open-hand conditions and two bonus transfers](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/781.html).
Declare ready before winning. A standard hand needs an open set, three suits, and a terminal or honor.
The completed hand needs a pung or kong, which may be completed by a wait on two pairs. Dragon eyes
can exempt this rule. Closed seven pairs can declare and win. In this room, seven pairs still require
the suit and terminal or honor conditions. Concealed kongs require an open hand. Chows come from the
preceding seat. The wall leaves 14 tiles at the tail.

Three identical first discards following the dealer's identical first discard transfer 5 from the dealer
to each opponent. An intervening call ends this opportunity. The middle-five bonus applies when the winning
5 can complete a 4–5–6 sequence in a legal shape. Add 10 to each self-draw bill, or 20 only to the supplier's
discard bill. The published extra payments are combined here with a base of 1 from each opponent, doubled
on self-draw or seven pairs. On a discard, an unready supplier doubles their base bill before the extra
payment. A ready supplier pays the usual base plus any middle-five fee. Added-kong robbery uses the same
supplier schedule. A completed kong pays 1/2 per opponent for open/concealed and stays paid on a draw.

## Laoyou Fuxin Hidden Ready

[Bianfeng's Laoyou guide](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/632.html) describes concealed ready, the minimum
winning amount and the unready supplier's liability. Pon and kong are available without chows. A standard
hand has four sets and a pair. There is no suit requirement. A ready player puts a tile face down. Only the
owner sees it. It can never be called and is stored apart from discards, including during settlement.

Award the rank of the winning tile on a discard or robbery, and twice that rank on a self-draw. The bill
must reach 6. An unready supplier alone pays three times the share. A ready supplier pays their own bill
while the other two opponents pay the same amount. Self-draw also charges every opponent. This room counts
an honor as 6, reserves 14 tiles and uses the same 1/2 kong schedule as the other Fuxin room. It keeps the
folded declaration private through the final hand display and treats a robbed kong as a discard for billing.
