# Jianchang and Liaoyang Split Mahjong

Both Dojo rooms use 136 tiles, four players, 13 tiles per nondealer, 14 for the dealer, and eight hands per match.
The dealer keeps the seat on a win or draw. Otherwise the seat passes in turn order. Nearby claims have priority for a single discard win.

## Jianchang Final Split

[Bianfeng's Jianchang rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/129.html) describe the three-suit, terminal-or-honor, open-hand and pung gates,
as well as the split at 20 tiles. Make a pung, kong or dragon pair alongside the other requirements. A dragon pung
requires an all-pung hand. Closed seven pairs can also win, using all three suits and at least one terminal or honor.

The Dojo room uses the option set with wind tiles, seven pairs, robbery of an added kong, gang points on a draw, and
three separate bills when a tile feeds a win. A middle wait or pair completion is an eye win. A winning tile that
supplies the sole natural terminal is also an eye win. Ordinary waits pay 1, eye waits 2, and all pungs and seven
pairs pay 4. Self-draw, a win on a split draw, a kong-replacement win, a win on the discard after a kong, and
robbery of an added kong each double the eligible base. The dealer, discarder, and closed payer multipliers apply
to each loser's bill where eligible. An added-kong robbery counts as a feeding discard.

When the wall reaches 20, the upcoming seat draws first. Each seat can keep one drawn tile aside and pass, or win
on it. Split tiles never enter the river or an opponent's visible tiles. On a draw the dealer pays each opponent 1.
Matching first discards in uninterrupted play also cost the dealer 1 per opponent. The source lists both side
payments but omits their directions. The Dojo table uses those dealer-to-opponent transfers. A fed kong charges only its supplier. A self-made added kong
charges each opponent 1, a concealed kong 2, and any dragon kong multiplies the corresponding bill by 4.
A fed kong uses one bill in this room. Reserve the final 16 tiles on a draw.

## Liaoyang Joker and Treasure

[The Liaoning Bianfeng rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/964.html) specify a restricted joker, split draws
beginning at 21 tiles, the post-win reveal and the full-treasure bonuses. They also describe a three-suit pung
gate with exceptions for all pungs and seven pairs, as well as fan for natural hands, concealed pungs, the
winning circumstances and features such as 2/8 eyes, a concealed dragon pung and four-of-a-kind outside a kong.

The Dojo room reveals a physical opening indicator and uses its remaining same-face tiles as jokers. A joker
cannot leave the hand through a discard or call, replace a numbered terminal, or count toward the natural
three-suit gate. A natural honor or terminal is required in every win. A fourth set is allowed only for all
pungs that already supply a natural honor or terminal, so a pair of jokers can always complete the remaining
eyes. Before settlement, the split resumes in seat order from the next draw when 21 tiles remain, with 17
set aside at a dry wall.

After a win reveal the next wall tile. Count the matching printed faces in the winning concealed and exposed
sets, including the claimed tile. One match adds one point per loser, three matches pay four, and two matching
joker faces also pay four. The treasure is separate from the 64 point cap on the fan subtotal. The opening
joker selection, flat treasure unit, bill cap, and the one-bill-per-loser default are Dojo conventions.
The source does not define the setup for gun tiles or opening rerolls. This room uses the base table with those
options off. Kongs award fan on eligible wins and have no separate payment.
