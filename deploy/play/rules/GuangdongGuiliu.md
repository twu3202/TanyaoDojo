# Golden Island Guangdong and Guiliu Mahjong

## Golden Island Guangdong / 黄金岛广东麻将

Rule ID `golden-island-guangdong-dojo-v1`. [Golden Island rules and scores](https://www.80166.com/guide/rule/gdmj.html).

- Four players, 136 tiles, no chow. The first dealer is random. Once the seats have 14/13/13/13 tiles, each picks
  any three of their own. All four passes to the next seat are simultaneous. The dealer's opening win is evaluated
  after exchange. Winner deals next, the provider takes over on multiple wins, and a draw retains the dealer.
- Standard hands, seven pairs and thirteen orphans can win. A plain chicken hand (2) or all-sequence hand (3)
  cannot win on an ordinary discard. Pass an offered win and only a higher base share may win until you draw or
  successfully claim a meld. More than one player may win on a single offer. Added kongs may be robbed;
  only thirteen orphans may rob a concealed kong.
- The base uses the highest compatible additive interpretation. Half flush 6, pure suit 12, all honors 20,
  mixed terminals and honors 12 or all terminals 16, all triplets 6, little/big dragons or winds 12/20,
  seven pairs 8/16/24/36 for zero to three quads, four kongs 36 and thirteen orphans 20. As in the chart,
  all honors excludes flush and mixed terminals, big dragons/winds excludes its smaller counterpart,
  the pairs grades are exclusive, and four kongs replaces all triplets. An opening win adds 20.
  Late self-draw, replacement self-draw and robbery modifiers add one each when applicable.
- Keep four horse tiles apart from the live wall. This room blocks all kongs and discard melds over the final
  four live positions. On a win reveal the four reserved tiles once; count their positions again for each winner.
  Starting with the winner, 1/5/9, East and Red hit the first seat; 2/6, South and Green the next;
  3/7, West and White the opposite; 4/8 and North the last. A payer's multiplier is one plus the sum of that
  payer's hits and the winner's hits. A 2-point draw with hit counts 2/1/1/0 pays 8/8/6.
- Self-draw bills the other three. An ordinary discard or robbed kong bills only the provider. On a direct-kong
  replacement win, the provider pays the sum of the three individually horse-adjusted shares. If there is no
  such cover, the supplier of the fourth open meld covers those same three shares. A robbed kong is cancelled.
- Direct kong charges 3 to its provider, added kong charges 1 to every opponent, concealed kong 2 to each.
  A draw refunds all kong payments. Four uninterrupted identical opening discards charge the dealer 1 per opponent.

## Guiliu Wake-Tile Mahjong / 桂柳上下醒麻将

Rule ID `guiliu-wake-dojo-v1`. [Guiliu scoring and reciprocity examples](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/1121.html).

- Four players, 136 tiles, no chow; pon and kong are available. Multiple discard wins are allowed.
  This room allows added-kong robbery as a discard win. Pass an offered win and only a higher known share may win
  before drawing or taking a meld. The highest-priority winner in seat order deals next; the dealer repeats on a draw.
- A ragged hand consists of 14 distinct tiles, at least five honors and gaps of at least three within
  each suit. All seven honors make it seven-star ragged. Standard hands and seven-pairs grades are also legal.
  The ordinary table uses dealer/nondealer discard bases of 7/5, or 10/8 for ragged hands. On self-draw,
  a dealer receives 4 each, or 15 each for ragged. A nondealer receives 4 from the dealer and 2 from
  the others, or 5 and 3 for ragged. The detailed dealer/nondealer table takes precedence over the later
  general base-point sentence in the source.
- Big hands use 15 per opponent on self-draw or 35 on a discard, times two to the power of total fan minus one.
  Seven-star ragged, pure suit, all honors, all triplets, four-open-set discard win and small seven pairs
  each count one, with one to three quads replacing small seven pairs with two, three or four. The 60-point
  pure-suit plus luxury-seven-pairs example is before closed-hand or event multipliers.
- Wake indicators count adjacent ranks of the same suit, without rank wrap, and adjacent winds or dragons
  within their separate cycles. Count matching physical tiles in the winning hand and its melds. Three or
  more matches on the current indicator extend the shared reveal by one tile if any remain. Each winner uses
  their own prefix and hit total. When the wall is empty, the winning tile itself is the single indicator.
  Each hit adds one to each self-draw share or three to the discard share, then closed-hand and kong-event
  doubles each apply if qualified. The room counts a concealed kong as closed.
- Three exposed feeds from one opponent create a direct reciprocal relationship, including direct kong.
  On self-draw, every partner pays twice the full three-seat bill and other seats are exempt. On a discard,
  the provider pays their share and each partner adds one. When linked seats win together, their mutual
  payments cancel if their adjusted amounts match. Otherwise they each pay the other's full share.
- Direct and added kongs bill their original supplier 3; concealed kong charges each opponent 2. Kong money
  stands on a draw. After identical first discards without a call, the first winner pays 2 to each other seat
  at settlement. A drawn hand has no winner to pay this opening charge.
