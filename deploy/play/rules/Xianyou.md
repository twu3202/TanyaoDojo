# Xianyou Mahjong

[Xinyue describes two profiles and their scoring schedules](https://xinyueyouxi.com/game/221-22?channel_id=0&product_id=33&region=).
[PonPon's player guide](https://www.xyfish.com/thread-178477-1-1.html) supplies a separate 112-tile self-draw game,
including the 16/17 deal, restrictions on calls, and gold hand payments.

## Play at all three tables

Nondealers start with 16 tiles and the dealer starts with 17. Pon comes from any discard. Direct, concealed
and added kongs are available. A standard win takes five sets and a pair. Keep gold tiles in the concealed
hand as wildcards. Gold cannot be discarded or called. Printed faces apply to calls and melds.

Gold travel is a separate discard choice. After the discard the hand must have five complete sets, including
melds, with a gold left to pair with any next draw. The traveler waits through opponents' turns and can win
on the next draw. Passing the draw and making an ordinary discard clears travel. The Xinyue tables pay the
three-gold rate when the travel began with at least three actual golds.

## White Dragon Substitution and White Dragon Flowers

Both Xinyue-based tables use the three suits, four White Dragons and eight flowers, for 120 tiles. Turn up
flowers in passes and replace from the tail. The next tile revealed from the live tail is an indicator.
Gold is the next rank in that suit, with nine wrapping to one. The visible indicator stays beside the wall.
This leaves all four copies of gold in circulation. At the opening, three or four held golds can win before
the indicator claim window. During that window, the indicator can stand as a borrowed gold for the win.
The dealer exchanges a discard for it. The two-gold pair award requires a complete self-drawn hand whose
pair is two actual golds and whose five sets need no other wildcards. It uses the regular draw rate.

White Dragon Substitution uses White Dragons as the natural value of gold in the concealed rack. An
ordinary win, gold claim or kong robbery is 2. Gold travel pays 4 or 6 and three or four held golds pay 3
or 10. Add 1 for each completed added kong and 2 for each concealed kong in the winning hand. A direct
kong transfers 3 from the supplier at once.

White Dragon Flowers exposes every White Dragon as a replacement flower. Start the win at base 2 with
one point per actual gold, one per replacement flower, one per direct or added kong and two per concealed
kong. A gold-free ordinary self-draw adds 3 to the base. Discard wins and kong robbery multiply the base by
1, ordinary self-draw and gold claim by 2, travel by 4 or 6, and three or four held golds by 3 or 10.

The discard supplier pays on a discard win, the added-kong supplier pays on a robbery, and all opponents
pay on a self-draw or gold win. Only added kongs can be robbed.

## 112-tile Self-Draw

The same three suits and four White Dragons form a 112-tile wall. All White Dragons are fixed gold. Pon
and kong are the available calls. Normal wins come from a draw. After the dealer's first discard, declare
three or four golds on your own draw or in the response to another player's discard. Each opponent pays
base 1, one per actual gold and per direct or added kong, and two per concealed kong. Travel counts 3 for
the first gold and 2 for each further gold in the winning rack. Four golds pay 10 flat.

## Dojo room conventions

The sources leave some play details open. These profiles choose a 12-tile reserve, eight hands, no chows,
a single winner, a fixed White Dragon gold face for the 112-tile table, and seat order from the dealer's
right for opening and discard responses. The dealer retains the seat on a draw or dealer win. The Xinyue
tables use the tile mix, indicator and temporary claim above. White Dragons replaced as flowers are worth
one flower each. Two-gold and three-gold travel eligibility, the flat 2-point ordinary win in the
substitution table, and the single billing of direct kongs are fixed as described above. A single player
may travel at a time. The travel stays active through a kong replacement and requires at least four live
tiles at declaration. Kong count refers to completed sets.
