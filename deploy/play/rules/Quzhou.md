# Quzhou Duanzi and Lata

The [Zhejiang Game Hall Quzhou room rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/93.html) list Duanzi, ordinary Lata and banked Lata. All use four seats and 136 tiles. The display tile is removed after the opening deal. The other copies of its face are wild. A White Dragon can keep its printed identity or stand for the flipped face inside a concealed winning shape. Calls use printed faces. Wildcards can be discarded but cannot be claimed. Kong replenishment comes from the live tile next to the reserve. This room treats kongs as replacement actions and awards no separate kong payment.

## Duanzi

Allow pon and kong and draw through the entire wall. The first accepting player wins. The winner deals next, or the dealer stays after a draw. A winning hand must qualify for one of the awards below. Plain four-set-and-a-pair hands need an additional qualifying pattern. The point columns apply to one opponent on self-draw or the supplier alone on a discard.

| Hand | Self-draw per opponent | Discard supplier |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Mixed suit | 2 | 4 |
| Pure suit, all pungs or seven pairs | 3 | 7 |
| Mixed-suit pungs or mixed-suit seven pairs | 4 | 10 |
| Pure-suit pungs or pure-suit seven pairs | 5 | 13 |
| Separated hand | 2 | 4 |
| Separated hand with all seven honors | 3 | 7 |
| All honors | 6 | 16 |
| All-honor pungs or all-honor seven pairs | 11 | 31 |
| Three wildcards or seven printed honors | 1 | Self-draw only |

For the separated hand, assign the rank groups 147, 258 and 369 to the three suits in any order. Use five to seven distinct honors and enough distinct tiles from those suited ranks to reach 14. The room allows wildcards to fill empty slots in this shape. The separate Seven-Honor self-draw award requires the seven printed honor faces. A robbed added kong pays twice the discard amount from its supplier. [Zhejiang Game Hall's Quzhou play guide](https://www.hzbianqu.com/article/1127.html)

## Ordinary Lata

Allow chow and stop with 34 tiles left. The dealer stays after winning or drawing, and otherwise passes the deal to the next seat. Plain standard hands and the Duanzi special hands can win. The flexible separated hand accepts any suited ranks at least three apart within each suit, plus distinct honors to complete 14 tiles. The highest eligible shape determines the displayed award. Ordinary bills use the main room's two categories and the special timing bills from the [published Lata guide](https://www.hzbianqu.com/article/1097.html).

- A plain self-draw bills each opponent 2. A special self-draw bills each opponent 4.
- The supplier pays 2 on a plain discard win and 4 on a special one. The uninvolved opponents each pay 1 less.
- A qualified dealer opening win, win on the first discard, replacement win, or four-set single wait bills each opponent 10. A robbed added kong bills its supplier 30.

## Banked Lata

Use Lata's tiles, calls, win conditions and dealer rotation. The published banked schedule instead sets each bill by position and mode of win. A robbed added kong uses the discard row with the player attempting the kong as the supplier.

| Win | Payers and amounts |
| --- | --- |
| Dealer self-draw | Three opponents pay 8 each |
| Nondealer self-draw | Dealer pays 8. Two other opponents pay 4 each |
| Dealer wins on a discard | Supplier pays 8. Two other opponents pay 4 each |
| Nondealer wins on the dealer's discard | Dealer pays 4. Two other opponents pay 1 each |
| Nondealer wins on another nondealer's discard | Supplier and dealer pay 2 each. Other opponent pays 1 |

Each player starts at 30 points. If any payer cannot cover a bill, the payer transfers their remaining points. The other payers settle their own bills normally and the match ends. The highest remaining balance wins. A balance can reach exactly zero without ending the match. The balance cap and the result display implement the published maximum loss of the initial 30 points. This room plays until an overdraft. Duanzi and ordinary Lata play eight hands.
