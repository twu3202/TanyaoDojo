# Tianjin and Beijing Mahjong

Both four-player rooms use 136 tiles and play eight hands. The public flip stays in the reserve. Winds cycle
East–South–West–North and dragons cycle Red–Green–White. Kongs draw from the live edge of the reserve.

## Tianjin Double Hun

[Laiyouxi's Tianjin rules](https://static.laiyouxi.com/games/6.html) use the flipped face and its successor as jokers.
Roll two dice and count that many stacks from the tail. In this room the reserve contains the flipped stack
and the stacks behind it. Nondealers set their stakes before the deal and before anybody sees the flip.
Choose 1, 2 or 4 for bills against the dealer. The dealer's ordinary ×2 still applies.
No stake multiplier applies to a bill between two nondealers.

Players can pon and kong using matching printed tiles. Both joker faces are protected against pon and open kong.
Discarding either joker seals that player's right to win for the rest of the hand.
Four physically identical jokers can make a golden concealed kong. See [91y's golden kong example](https://mj.91y.com/mjjq/844.html).
The kong charge to each opponent is 1 for exposed or added, 2 for concealed, and 8 for golden, before the
dealer and raise factors. All completed kongs count even on a draw. Four matching uninterrupted opening
discards charge the dealer 1 to each guest as a separate bill.

A win needs four groups and a pair and must be self-drawn. A one-point basic hand does not win by itself.
A hand without physical jokers earns 2. To qualify for a single-joker wait, the pre-draw hand must have
four complete groups and a joker available for the eyes. A double-joker wait needs three complete groups
and eyes, leaving two available jokers for the final group. Both are worth 2. Catch-five is worth 3 and uses
the winning tile as the center of 4–5–6 characters. A joker may represent the 5, or fill either missing side.
A 1–9 straight is worth 4 and requires 123, 456 and 789 of one suit in a valid decomposition. A straight
in a joker suit doubles again. Catch-five and the straight must fit the same hand partition and combine as
7, whether the draw completes the straight or a separate 456.
[Patterns](https://static.laiyouxi.com/skill/723.html) and
[combination examples](https://static.laiyouxi.com/skill/724.html) show the waits and stacking.

Kong replacement doubles the win. An opening dealer win adds 8 to a valid shape. The winner receives the
individual bills from all three opponents. The winner deals next, and the dealer stays after a draw.

## Beijing Upstairs Hun

[JJ Beijing's rulebook](https://www.jj.cn/news/320/20120716143900024737.shtml) uses only the flip's successor as a joker.
This room rolls one die and reserves through the indicated stack. The joker cannot be discarded or used for
a printed meld. A player may chow, pon or kong normally with natural tiles.
After a chow, pon or open kong, or with at least three jokers in the winning rack, a player can win only by
self-draw. A concealed kong preserves closed status. Four physical jokers on a draw force a 20-point win
with the dealer factor suppressed. A player may rob an added kong when eligible to claim. Robbing a concealed
kong requires the taken face to complete a 1–9 straight. The first eligible claim in order wins.

A basic hand consists of four groups and one pair. Seven pairs is also allowed. The win starts at 1.
No jokers, closed status, all pungs and the ordinary straight each double their eligible shape.
A pure numbered suit quadruples the score. For an ordinary straight, the three sequences can include
declared chows. A single joker used in the straight makes the enhanced straight at ×4.
Catching the 5 characters needs an actual middle 5 as the unique structural wait, or a seven-pairs win on that
tile. Seven pairs is ×2 with no printed quad and doubles for each printed quad. Kong replacement quadruples
and robbing a kong doubles. Play in the last 18 physical wall tiles, including the reserve, awards ×2.
Heavenly and Earthly Wins take the standalone 20-point award in this room.

On a self-draw each opponent pays. Each bill involving the dealer doubles except on the four-joker win.
On a discard or robbed kong, the supplier pays the sum of all three bills. Only the winner collects
completed-kong bonuses, with 1 for an exposed or added kong and 2 for a concealed kong from each opponent.
These bonus amounts are a Dojo room convention and are tracked separately.

Four identical discards in a dealer-led round with no intervening call burn the hand after response claims.
Three count when the wall runs out before the fourth player can draw. A burned hand counts as a draw.
The next hand after a draw or the fourth consecutive hand with the same dealer is upstairs.
Upstairs doubles both the winning and kong bills. The winner deals next after a win and the dealer stays on a draw.
