# Bengbu and Lipu Mahjong

The source for both Bengbu tables is the [operator’s two Bengbu rules profiles](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/894.html).
The Lipu table follows the [operator’s Lipu rules and payout examples](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/1146.html).
Each Dojo match plays eight hands with four seats. The player at seat zero deals first.

## Bengbu

Both rooms start with 136 tiles and allow pon, kong, ordinary discard wins and robbery of an added kong.
A win requires four sets and a pair. A concealed kong shows one tile. After declining a win or pon, the
same class of claim is blocked until the player's next draw or an accepted call. The first accepting winner
in order after the supplier wins. The dealer remains after a dealer win or draw and otherwise passes to
the next seat.

| Room | Winning discard | Self-draw per payer | Added kong robbery | Wall |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Classic | 1 | 2 | Kong owner covers the winner's three self-draw shares | Leave 14 |
| East Wind Flowers | 1 | 2 | Kong owner pays one discard share | Exhaust the wall |

Only the winner's kongs score. Concealed kongs add two each. Exposed kongs add one, with two per payer
on an East Wind Flowers self-draw. East Wind Flowers adds one for each exposed flower. Each bill between
the winner and a losing seat adds one if either is the dealer. Classic kong-draw wins double the hand
value before the dealer surcharge. Classic robbery sums the three bills with their dealer surcharges.
This table leaves the optional post-kong discard multiplier off. East Wind Flowers uses no kong-draw
multiplier.

East and the three Dragons are flowers. The dealer replaces flowers before the first discard. An
opponent holds their dealt flowers in the rack until their first ordinary draw. An early pon or kong
does not expose them. If an early replacement draw is itself a flower, it stays in the rack too. At
the seat's first ordinary draw, it takes the head tile, exposes its flowers and replaces from the tail
until it has ordinary tiles or the wall is exhausted. Flowers can't be discarded, called or included in a win.

## Lipu

Use 136 tiles with chows, pons, kongs and seven pairs. This room enables added kongs with robbery and the
chow swap restriction. It disables bonus horses and seat horses. A single winner becomes the next dealer.
The supplier deals after multiple wins on one tile. The dealer stays after an exhaustive draw.

- Three exposed sets supplied to the same seat link the pair. Each partner matches a distinct supplier's
  bill on a discard-style win. On ordinary self-draw the partners cover all three shares. For the rare
  case of multiple linked seats, split the full self-draw bill among them with any remainder assigned in
  clockwise order from the winner. A seat that is both the supplier and a partner pays its single bill.
- Four exposed sets or three pons to the same recipient block the supplier's wins. A pon upgraded to a
  kong still counts as that pon. Sealed seats can play and call, and their kongs completed beforehand
  stay on the ledger. Kongs completed while sealed earn zero.
- A direct or added kong earns one from the discard or original pon supplier. A concealed kong earns one
  from each opponent. These transfers stay on a draw. A kong-draw win or a win on the following discard
  reverses the latest kong transfer once, even for multiple winners. Robbing an added kong interrupts it
  before its transfer, and earlier kong transfers stay.

| Shape | Discard | Self-draw per payer |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Plain | 1 | 1 |
| All pons, one suit, small tow truck, seven pairs | 3 | 2 |
| Big tow truck, luxury seven pairs | 6 | 4 |
| Super luxury seven pairs | 12 | 8 |

A tow truck hand is down to its final concealed pair after four melds. With a chow it is small, and
without a chow it is big. In the latter case it already embodies an all-pons shape. Compatible shape
awards use the largest value and double for each additional award. For example, the operator’s
one-suit big tow truck on a discard pays 12, using 6 doubled once. Luxury seven pairs uses one quad
and super luxury uses at least two.

An ordinary self-draw without exposed melds doubles; concealed kongs keep the hand closed. A concealed
kong draw uses triple self-draw points. Robbery, an exposed kong draw, or the discard immediately after
an exposed kong uses seven times the discard value. A discard immediately after a concealed kong uses
nine times the discard value. The operator's table names the payer for a direct kong draw. The room
uses the original pon supplier in the corresponding added-kong case.
