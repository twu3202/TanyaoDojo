# Panjin and Anshan Mahjong

These rooms use 136 tiles, four seats and eight-hand matches. The room reserves a physical indicator and sets the
joker to its successor. Numbered suits wrap from nine to one, winds use East–South–West–North, and dragons use
Red–Green–White. The public indicator and joker are visible throughout a hand. Jokers are kept in the hand
during ordinary play and never fill an exposed call. A compulsory joker discard from an all-joker rack is
dead to other players. On a win by the dealer or on a draw the seat repeats. Otherwise the dealer advances.

## Panjin Joker Kong

[Bianfeng's Panjin rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/809.html) describe the successor joker, closed and open
wins, three natural suits, the restriction on using jokers as terminals or pungs, the four-joker kong, the
split starting with 12 tiles and the discarder's liability.

The Dojo room uses successor jokers and enables kongs, joker kongs, seven pairs, all pungs, the no-joker bonus
and the One Bamboo bonuses. It uses the three-suit option. A natural terminal or honor is required.
A standard hand needs a completed natural pung, kong or honor pair. A joker can fill a numbered chow or the
eyes but cannot replace a numbered terminal or any pung tile. Seven pairs can fill a non-terminal pair with a
joker. A declared joker kong fills a set and contributes neither a suit nor a terminal or honor to the rack gate.

An open kong charges each opponent 1, a concealed kong charges each opponent 2, and a matching four-joker kong
charges each opponent 64. The room retains these payments on a draw. Split tiles allow self-draw or pass. A kong
claim on the last discard before the split is still available and its replacement is the first split tile. The wall
keeps eight reserved tiles on a draw. The room uses one closest discard winner and ordinary, non-robbed kongs.
The dead-discard escape in the source's two-joker option also applies to an all-joker rack in this room.

Win payments start at one point per opponent and double for self-draw or discard win, natural concealed tiles
without jokers, all pungs or seven pairs, a middle wait, and the listed eligible kong circumstances. One Bamboo
doubles the base and four printed One Bamboo copies quadruple it. A joker of that face still counts. The dealer
doubles the applicable bill. On a discard win the supplier covers the sum of all three bills. This is the Dojo
interpretation of the source's combined dealer, discard and three-bill rules.

## Anshan Open Guns

[Bianfeng's Anshan rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/957.html) describe the successor joker, natural
suit and terminal conditions, gun choices, and the extra bill for the discarder or all three self-draw opponents.

The Dojo room selects the open-gun option. If the joker is numbered, its suit's seven is the public open gun.
Each printed copy in the winning hand or its melds adds one fan. A printed copy counts even if the same face
also acts as the joker. An honor joker sets no gun. The dark-gun and post-win gun options are disabled
for this room. The starting indicator is selected from the wall by the seeded shuffle. An all-joker discard
disables wins for the discarder for the rest of that hand.

For an ordinary win, keep at least one natural terminal or honor and show all three suits in natural tiles.
A closed pung, exposed pung, kong or dragon eyes satisfies the pung condition. With the natural terminal or
honor present, the joker can supply another terminal. Seven pairs and thirteen orphans are also available.
Seven pairs can omit a suit and thirteen orphans requires all three suits to be represented naturally.
The 14-tile tail, standing fan for a closed winner, last-live self-draw bonus, and added-kong robbery treated
as a discard win are Dojo choices. Scoring also awards fan for a natural hand, kongs in the winning set,
kong draws, dragon pungs, three pungs, 2/8 eyes and printed four-in-one outside a kong. All losers pay, with
one dealer factor where eligible plus a discard factor for the supplier or a self-draw factor for all three.
