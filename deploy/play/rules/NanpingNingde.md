# Nanping and Ningde Sixteen-Tile Mahjong

All four tables deal 16 tiles per nondealer and 17 to the dealer. They use eight-hand matches
and five sets plus a pair for ordinary wins. Chows come from the preceding seat. Pon, direct
kongs and discard wins can come from any other seat. A discard or added kong has at most one
winner. The dealer stays on a draw or dealer win and advances otherwise.

## Nanping

[Tongchengyou's Nanping rules](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d13231.html) describe the deal, flower passes
and White Dragon substitution. The [three Nanping sixteen-tile score tables](https://www.xinyueyouxi.com/game/223-22?doc-innerlink-%E5%B1%85%E6%B0%91=&region=)
come from Xinyue. The Dojo tile mixes and wall reserves below are fixed room conventions for these profiles.

| Table | Tile supply | Gold | Reserve | Direct kong |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Honors and Flowers | 108 suited, 28 honors, 8 flowers | First nonflower flipped after the flower passes | 16 | 1 from supplier |
| Suits and Flowers | 108 suited, 8 flowers | Suited face flipped after the flower passes | 12 | 3 from supplier |
| Red Dragon Gold | 108 suited, 4 Red Dragons, 8 flowers | All four Red Dragons | 12 | 1 from supplier |

- Reveal flowers in passes starting with the dealer. Take replacements immediately outside the reserve.
  In a flipped room, expose additional flowers reached before the indicator. The indicator stays off the
  live wall. In the honors room, White Dragon supplies the original gold face in concealed scoring.
  Calls still use the printed faces. Gold itself cannot be chowed or ponned.
- Dealer heaven has first priority in the opening. In the flipped tables, dealt three golds then take priority
  over taking the indicator. A nondealer adds the indicator to a complete ready rack. The dealer exchanges
  one tile. The fixed-gold table offers an opening four-gold choice regardless of seat, with dealer heaven
  ahead and a first-discard win ahead of the nondealer's three-gold declaration.
- The Nanping tables use whole-turn passed-win and passed-pon locks, cleared by a draw. An opening
  follow-discard streak uses printed faces. Four identical discards in order charge the dealer two per
  opponent for a number or one for an honor. Each consecutive matching round doubles the payment.
  A mismatch or a call ends the streak.
- In the suited room, choose the listed special value or the ordinary base of one on a discard and
  two on self-draw. Honors and fixed red use a one-point base, each held gold, the dealer-repeat count,
  and each flower. Four matching plant or season flowers contribute six in total. Double that subtotal
  on self-draw and add the highest listed award. The kong ledger posts separately, including on a draw,
  with two from each opponent for concealed kong and one for an added kong.
- The fixed red profile additionally accepts seven pairs with a pung and allows declaring the four Red
  Dragons as a kong. The other two gold rooms use the displayed indicator with only three live golds.

## Ningde White Gold

The [developer’s Ningde description](https://app-1218204345.appstor.io/) specifies a 112-tile
16/17-tile deal, fixed White Dragons and the difference payments. The [Yiran Ningde and Fu’an schedule](https://www.al173.com/index-3.htm)
gives the awards for special hands.

- Use the 108 numbered tiles and four White Dragons. Each White Dragon is gold. Keep a twelve-tile
  reserve. Besides an ordinary hand, seven pairs and a pung can win. Three and four golds can win
  on their own, and four golds can be declared as a kong. The initial priority is dealer heaven,
  four golds, a first-discard win, then three golds.
- Ordinary wins are worth two plus the winner’s held gold and kong units on a discard, doubled
  on self-draw. Use the highest special value when applicable. A bill involving the dealer adds
  one. The discarder or kong supplier pays one bill; self-draw charges each opponent. In a pure
  suit, three exposed feeds from one opponent redirect the discard bill to them. Four such feeds
  make them pay all self-draw bills. Concealed kongs are excluded from feeds.
- When one seat wins, each pair of nonwinners compares their combined held gold and completed
  kong units. The lower value pays the higher the difference. Count one per held White Dragon,
  one for each exposed or added kong, and two per concealed kong. A completed White Dragon kong
  also counts its four golds. Drawn hands have no side transfers.

## Gold hand conventions

The gold pair uses two golds as the eyes. The gold group sets aside three actual golds as one
complete set, then checks that the remaining tiles can win. The all-chow route earns the gold
dragon award where the schedule lists it separately. Pure and mixed suit patterns use the
natural faces after any printed substitution, and permit golds to fill missing tiles. A gold
kong requires a completed four-tile declaration. A robbed added kong settles as a discard win
without paying a completed-kong award.
