# Quanzhou and Ningde Gold Mahjong

The two tables share a 16/17 deal, five sets and a pair, three suits and the seven ordinary honors,
eight flowers, and eight-hand matches. Flowers are replaced in passes. The first nonflower flipped
from the live tail is gold and stays on display, leaving three matching tiles in play. Discarded gold
cannot be taken. Chows come from the preceding player. Discard wins and added-kong robbery charge
the supplier. Self-draws charge every opponent. Each hand has at most one winner.

## Quanzhou

[Xinyue publishes the scoring and travel table](https://www.xinyueyouxi.com/game/219-22?doc-innerlink-%E6%97%A0%E6%95%8C=&region=).
[Tiandu describes the 144-tile wall and winner-dealer progression](https://m.tdqp8.com/Home/Info/10652.html).
[The local travel account describes immediate third travel](https://kknews.cc/zh-sg/news/rj2azvx.html).
The wall keeps 16 tiles at the tail. The winner deals the next hand and the dealer stays on a draw.
Use the published additive repeat count. It applies only to a bill between the dealer and another seat.

The winning subtotal starts at 1 with one per flower and one per held gold. Pungs count 1 for a numbered
face or 2 for an honor. Direct and added kongs count 2 if numbered or 3 if honors in the set subtotal.
Concealed kongs count 3 or 4. The kong subtotal awards another 1 for an addition, 2 for a direct kong,
and 3 for a concealed kong. A gold can complete a pung. When multiple decompositions win, take the
highest possible set subtotal. Gold assignments may repeat a face. A special win without a completed
shape counts the actual natural pungs already present in the rack.

Multiply the subtotal by 1 on a discard or added-kong robbery, 2 on self-draw, 3 with three golds,
and 4 on a dealer win on the initial deal. This room also awards eight flowers at ×4. Play the
published ×3/×6/×9 set of travel multipliers. To begin, discard to five sets with at least one gold
available to pair with any draw. The first travel continues when the traveler draws and discards a new
gold. During double travel only self-draw wins are available and all discard or kong replies pass.
Drawing and discarding a further gold settles the third travel immediately, using the waiting rack and
held golds after that discard. The traveler can end the declaration by making an ordinary discard.

## Ningde Random Gold and Flowers

[Xinyue lists the random-gold scoring separately](https://www.xinyueyouxi.com/game/222-22?doc-innerlink-%E8%81%8C%E4%B8%9A=&region=).
[The older 91.com launch describes White Dragon substitution](https://news.17173.com/content/2005-7-29/n11_927315.html).
Dojo uses the 144-tile flower wall for the newer published schedule and adopts the older substitution
rule. White Dragons provide the gold's natural face inside the hand unless White Dragon is itself gold.
Exposed melds use printed faces. Keep 12 tiles at the tail. A draw or dealer win keeps the dealer in place.

The dealer can accept heaven first, followed by seats holding three golds and then by claims using the
visible indicator. The dealer must discard a tile to claim the indicator. A declared gold travel offers
the next self-draw with the ordinary draw multiplier. It is an optional wait with no extra award in
this profile. The common payment is one water per flower, held gold, direct or added kong, and two per
concealed kong. Add the repeat-dealer count. Double this subtotal on self-draw and add only the highest
special award. The schedule is 30 for heaven, claim or no water, 15 for one water, 40 for three golds,
60 for a two-gold pair, 120 for a gold triplet with all remaining sets as sequences or for mixed one
suit, and 240 for pure one suit. No water and one water count only the indicated flower, gold and kong
units, before the dealer repeat count.

## Dojo room conventions

The Quanzhou room fixes White Dragon as an ordinary honor unless flipped, the two kong contributions,
the eight-flower multiplier, the repeat-dealer bill, and the response rules above. It uses the dealer-win
and three-gold opening windows. It uses no separate indicator-claim window. A final eighth flower
that consumes the last live tile still scores. The Ningde room fixes the random-gold wall, water
definitions, ordinary travel multiplier, and printed-meld treatment described above. Its payments
go through the winner. The 112-tile Ningde White Gold table has its own fixed gold and loser transfers.
