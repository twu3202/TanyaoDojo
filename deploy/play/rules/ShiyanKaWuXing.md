# Shiyan Ka Wu Xing

The [Shiyan operator rules](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d30413.html) specify a three-player 84-tile game,
plain hands by self-draw, multiple winners, and keeping kong payments on a draw. This room uses the
same hand decomposition and call engine as the other [Ka Wu Xing rooms](KaWuXing.md).

- Use four of each dot, bamboo and dragon. Random first dealer, 14 tiles to the dealer and 13 to the
  other seats. A completed four-set hand and pair or seven pairs may win. You may pon and kong.
- A plain hand wins only by self-draw, at one point per payer. A qualifying pattern may win on a
  discard. Here middle-five means completing 4–5–6 with the winning 5 and doubles the hand.
  A sequence that contains a 5 elsewhere in the hand earns no bonus.
- The operator gives examples of patterns and no full multiplier table. This room uses the shared
  family values for other shapes, including pure suit ×4, all triplets ×2, and seven pairs ×4 with
  a double for each concealed quad. Use uncapped multiplication, with the middle-five value above.
- A replacement-draw win, robbery of an added kong, last-live-tile self-draw and a win on the
  immediate post-kong discard each double the scoring hand. The last two event bonuses apply to their
  respective self-draw and discard events. A post-kong discard can qualify for a win through its event double.
- Both opponents pay one share on self-draw. The supplier pays every winner on a discard or a robbed
  added kong. A single winner deals next; the supplier deals on a double win. Passes do not add a win lock.
- Kong payments are direct 2 from the supplier, added 1 from each opponent, concealed 2 from each opponent.
  These are fixed room choices. No kong without a replacement tile. Resolve pon or wins on the last discard.
  If play ends without winners, the dealer repeats and the kong ledger stands. This room uses no open-ready declaration.
