# Xiaogan Ka Wu Xing · Piao and horse

The room follows the [Xiaogan scoring description](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d25805.html)
for chows, single winners, open ready and horse play. The [operator's opening guide](https://m.tcy365.com/news/d26962.html)
gives the four piao choices. That guide describes another call option, so this room specifies which rules it uses.

- Three seats play with 84 tiles, comprising dots, bamboo and the three dragons. Each chooses zero to three
  piao before seeing the hand or dealer. The table reveals all choices together. They last until hand settlement.
- Chow from the previous seat; pon and kong from either other seat. At most one winner can take a tile,
  with the nearest accepting seat first. Added kongs may be robbed. The winner deals next, or the dealer repeats on a draw.
- Use Ka Wu Xing scoring patterns, with middle-five (the winning 5 in a 4–5–6 sequence) doubling the hand.
  Seven pairs with one or more quads uses an eightfold base. Seven pairs containing all three dragon pairs
  scores the ordinary seven-pairs value unless a quad gives the higher base. A dealer's opening self-draw doubles.
- Open ready reveals the hand after a ready discard and locks play to the drawn discard or a win.
  Open ready doubles the winning value. For an open-ready discard winner, the supplier pays two shares even
  if both seats revealed. A dealer badge does not affect who gets this payment. An ordinary discard win
  or robbery bills only the supplier. Both opponents pay on self-draw.
- The payer contributes their own piao plus the winner's piao once. For example, a winner on 3 and a payer
  on 2 add 5 to their existing bill. An open-ready self-draw with a tile still in the wall flips the wall tail as
  a horse. Each payer adds that tile's rank, with dragons fixed at 10 in this room.
- Kong payments use 2 from a direct supplier, 1 from each opponent for an added kong and 2 each for a
  concealed kong. Kong payments stand after a draw. A draw produces no piao, horse or first-reveal payment.
  The web result shows the horse and the hand, kong, piao and horse entries.
