# JJ Three-Player · Call Points

Use the [JJ 2017 three-player score table](https://www.jj.cn/help/new_74327.shtml). JJ also lists
a [2016 multiplier-based room](https://www.jj.cn/help/new_72708.shtml) with a distinct call and reveal system.
This room follows the 2017 score table.

- Three seats, 84 tiles. Play with characters, dots, and the three dragons. First dealer is random;
  the dealer receives 14 tiles and the other players receive 13. A four-set hand and pair or seven pairs
  may win at the same fixed value.
- A win takes priority over melds. Kong and pon precede chow. Only the next seat may chow. For a
  chow or pon that succeeds, the supplier immediately pays 1 or 2 respectively. There is no payment for
  an offer that was passed or superseded. A direct kong pays 5 from the supplier, an added kong pays 1
  from each opponent, and a concealed kong pays 5 from each opponent.
- A player may declare ready on a discard that leaves a structurally complete wait. Two opponents
  pay 2 each immediately and the declaration costs no points. The hand stays private and locked;
  the player can win or discard the drawn tile. A declaration discard can be won. Its ready payments stand.
- Each win pays 5 per payer. Both opponents pay on self-draw. The discarder or robbed added-kong actor
  pays 5 to each winner when one tile makes two wins. A robbed kong earns no kong payments. A single winner
  deals next; the supplier deals after multiple wins.
- Room choices include no new calls on the last discard except wins, no temporary passed-win lock,
  keeping all earned points on a draw, and the same dealer after a wall-exhaustion draw.
  The result itemizes chow, pon, kong and ready points separately from win payments.
