# Dalian Qionghu and Tieling Closed Ready

Both rooms use 136 tiles, four seats, and eight hands per match. The dealer stays after their own win
or a draw and otherwise advances one seat. Both use the closest eligible winner on a discard.

## Dalian Qionghu Private Bao

[Bianfeng's Dalian Qionghu rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/850.html) specify an open hand,
all three numbered suits, a terminal or honor, and at least one chow and a pung, kong or dragon
pair. Four declared sets are ineligible and the room disallows calls and concealed kongs that
would create a fourth set. Concealed kongs need an existing open meld.

Ordinary wins can occur without a declaration. Declaring ordinary ready locks every legal wait.
Declaring edge/middle ready locks just the waits with a 1–2 toward 3, an 8–9 toward 7, or a middle
completion. A declared hand discards the drawn tile and can kong only when the declared waits
are preserved. An eligible edge/middle-ready win earns one fan.

After declaring, look at the bao privately before the next personal draw. The reserve holds
14 tiles. For each of the first six stacks the fixed face is the first tile of its pair. The
Dojo die samples those stacks whose face has been discarded fewer than three times. Revealing
a bao that matches a wait offers an immediate win before drawing. The physical indicator stays
in the wall. A matching face physically drawn later can fill any locked wait. On the third
actual discard of a bao face it expires, even when one of those discards was claimed into a meld.
The next ready draw selects and privately checks a new eligible stack. No seat sees a new bao
before its own check.

The source gives the matching-bao bonus and states who pays. The Dojo room uses base 1, one fan
for self-draw or discard, one for a winning edge/middle declaration, and two for matching or
drawing bao. Double the relevant bill when the dealer is involved, when the payer is closed,
and when all three losing players are closed. The supplier pays just their own bill after a
discard or robbed added kong. Self-draw and bao wins charge all opponents. Kongs have no
immediate cash transfer. Replacement draws come from the playable edge adjacent to the reserve.

## Tieling Closed Ready

[Bianfeng's Tieling rules](https://www.hzbianqu.com/game/132.html) provide the closed-ready,
whirlwind, split, dealer, and longmao fan rules. This room enables closed ready. The open
route needs no declaration. The rack needs all three suits and a natural terminal or honor.
An ordinary hand needs a pung, kong, opening egg or dragon eyes. Only an all-pung hand
may win with four declared sets. This room uses the three-suit standard route.

Closed wins require a ready declaration and earn one fan. A drawn kong that preserves every
wait keeps that status. A closed seat must declare ready before making a concealed kong.
The kong may show its faces without opening the hand. During the first turn,
four winds or three dragons may be declared as an egg without opening the hand. The four-wind
egg receives a replacement. An egg earns two fan according to the settlement table.
A dragon pung earns one fan, and another seat's dragon egg adds two more. Another seat's
wind egg adds one fan to each wind pung. The room uses these as separate factors and reveals
declared kong faces for scoring. Added kongs can be robbed as discards.

A standard win starts at 1, a middle, edge or pair wait starts at 2, all pungs starts at 8
on a pung wait or 16 on the lone pair. Four declared pungs and a numbered pair in the remaining
suit add one fan. The four final tiles allow self-draw or pass and the pre-split boundary
closes chow, pon and kong calls. An uninterrupted match of all four first discards or a draw
on the previous hand adds one longmao fan. The two triggers share a single bonus.

Kongs and the longmao trigger add no immediate transfers. The Dojo room uses three separate
bills on an ordinary or discard win. The supplier's own bill gains one fan on a discard win.
The dealer factor, each closed payer's factor and the extra factor when all three opponents are
closed apply separately. Self-draw, a split win and the eligible kong circumstances add their
scoring factors to the winning hand. Pure one suit and seven pairs are optional source-room
settings outside this fixed room.
